These take an additional m before nouns ending in -nemą and w before nouns ending in -ne; neither is added with -na.\n\nThe verb prefix reflects both subject number and object number/class. The examples establish:\n• Singular subject + singular person/animal object: no prefix.\n• Plural subject + singular person/animal object: i-.\n• Singular subject + plural person/animal object or singular thing object: i-.\n• Plural subject + plural person/animal object or singular thing object: ipi-.\n• Plural subject + plural thing object: iw-.\n\nFor example, imt'oynemą is i-m-t'oy-nemą ‘their people’, and iwhųmų is iw-hų-mų ‘saw the cedars’, giving ‘Their people saw the cedars.’"
