{"j.":"Verb structure: SUBJECT–TENSE/ASPECT/POLARITY–VOICE–STEM.\n\nSubject prefixes are aɣa- ‘I’, a- ‘he/it’ and i- ‘they’. These remain present even when a noun subject is stated separately.\n\nTense/aspect/polarity markers are: no marker for affirmative past, te- for future, b- for ongoing action, ne- for negative past, and se- for negative ongoing/nonpast action.\n\nVoice is unmarked for ordinary active verbs. The prefix t- makes a passive, while s- makes a causative (‘have somebody do something’). The causative prefix harmonizes with a sibilant in the stem: it becomes z- with z and š- with š; consequently, ʒ- is expected with ʒ in iʒuwenket.\n\nSeveral verbs have different ordinary active and passive/causative stems: gon → elmez ‘swallow’, dini → ubuz ‘take’, kaw → ukuš ‘take out’, dumbu → egzem ‘slaughter’, and nin → išu ‘drink’. Other stems illustrated are egmi ‘look for’, uswud ‘watch’, and efred ‘gather’.\n\nPhrase structure is (subject noun)–verb–(object). An explicit subject precedes the verb; objects follow it. Object pronouns include a ‘him/it’ and i ‘them’. In passive sentences, the affected thing is the subject. The person performing a causatively arranged action can remain unspecified.\n\n5.1:\na. ‘The water was not drunk.’ anetišu = a-ne-t-išu: third-person singular, negative past, passive, ‘drink’.\nb. ‘I had the sheep watched.’ aɣasuswud = aɣa-s-uswud: first-person singular, past, causative, ‘watch’.\nc. ‘The salt will be swallowed.’ atetelmez = a-te-t-elmez: third-person singular, future, passive, ‘swallow’.\nd. ‘He is not taking the corpse.’ asedini = a-se-dini: third-person singular, negative nonpast, active ‘take’.\n\n5.2:\ne. absubuz aryen — absubuz = a-b-s-ubuz: ‘He is having the water taken.’\nf. aɣabʒiʒuwenket i — aɣabʒiʒuwenket = aɣa-b-ʒ-iʒuwenket: ‘I’m having them walked.’\ng. amanokal anenin aryen — anenin = a-ne-nin: ‘The chief did not drink the water.’\nh. cidi anetegmi — anetegmi = a-ne-t-egmi: ‘The salt was not looked for.’\ni. atesefred cidi — atesefred = a-te-s-efred: ‘He will have the salt gathered.’"}
