{"d.":"Northern Pame uses base 8: ‘a tenhiuɲ b’ means 8a + b. In the eights position, 1, 2 and 3 take the special forms kara, kanuje and karnuʔ. Central Pame uses base 20, with an intermediate grouping of 10: ‘a lien b’ means 20a + b, while seskaʔai means 10 and is followed by the units for 11–19. Central Pame also forms 8 as nda ntsawʔ and 9 as nda ntsawʔ nda. Thus Northern Pame groups by eights, whereas Central Pame groups by twenties and tens."}
