Thus biking/teacher is at 3, and the dancer is at 5. By clue 103, Barbara must be to the right of the biker, so Barbara must be at 4 or 5. But Wang is at 4; and Mandy must be at 1 because ligers must be between Mandy and the biker. Since Yair is at an end, Yair must be at 5. That leaves no possible position for Barbara.
