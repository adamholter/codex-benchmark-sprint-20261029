{"d.":"Northern Pame uses base 8: a number is expressed as a multiple of eight followed by the remainder, in the form ‘q tenhiuɲ r’ (= 8q + r). The coefficients 1–3 have special forms: kara, kanuje and karnuʔ.\n\nCentral Pame uses base 20: ‘q lien r’ means 20q + r. Within each group of twenty, seskaʔai means 10 and is followed by units to form 11–19. Thus Northern Pame groups numbers in eights, while Central Pame groups them in twenties, with an intermediate grouping of ten."}
