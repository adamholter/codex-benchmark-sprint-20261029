**(G) The Fellowship of the Ring**

The discussions and periods of inactivity don’t change who holds a book. After cancelling the swaps that undo one another, the effective swaps, in order, are:

1. Bob–Gertrude
2. Eve–Dave
3. Alice–Gertrude
4. Eve–Alice
5. Fred–Eve
6. Alice–Bob
7. Claire–Alice

After the first swap, Bob has *The Fellowship of the Ring*. None of the intervening effective swaps involves Bob, so he passes it to Alice in the sixth swap. Alice then passes it to Claire in the final swap.
